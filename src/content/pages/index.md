# Compassionate Senior Transition Solutions & Real Estate Liquidity

### Serving Northern California families and providing trusted, full-service transition support for elder law attorneys, senior living directors, social workers, and healthcare professionals.

When aging in place is no longer safe or sustainable, navigating the physical, financial, and logistical hurdles of moving to senior care can bring a family's plans to a complete halt.

**Newtree Senior Transition Services** serves as a single-source operational partner for families and professional advisors. We bridge the critical gap between healthcare, senior living, and real estate—handling everything from community placement and move management to solving complex, non-move-in-ready property situations.

---

> ### Why We Started: Closing the Regional Gap

> *Historically, families north of the Bay Area and Sacramento have lacked access to integrated, full-service senior transition specialists who can manage both senior placement and complex real estate liquidation under one roof. We stepped in to solve this exact problem for Northern California—providing local seniors, families, and healthcare providers with a dedicated, end-to-end partner who handles the hard work from start to finish.*

---

## Our Core Philosophy

* **Aging in Place First:** We believe that aging in place in one's own home is always the ideal scenario whenever possible. However, when independent living is no longer safe—and when home care or family support resources are exhausted—transitioning to a fully staffed, supportive community becomes the best path forward for safety and dignity.  
* **Serving Every Family:** We assist both **Private Pay and Medi-Cal (Medicaid) clients**. Financial background should never dictate whether a senior receives compassionate, high-quality guidance during a life transition.

## Services Overview

### 🏡 1\. Senior Living Placement & Navigation

Choosing a care community is about finding the exact match for medical needs, lifestyle, budget, and location.

* **Comprehensive Guidance:** We guide families to reputable local assisted living, memory care, board-and-care homes, and skilled nursing facilities.  
* **Private Pay & Medi-Cal Support:** Whether navigating private funds, long-term care insurance, or Medi-Cal placement programs, we help families find appropriate options.  
* **Streamlined Process:** We organize tours, compile paperwork, and facilitate communication between families and admissions teams.  
* **No Fee to Families:** Placement advisory services are provided at no cost to the senior or their family. 👉 [Learn More About Senior Placement Services →](http:///placement)

### 🔑 2\. Real Estate & Capital Solutions (As-Is Focus)

Most senior-owned homes are lived-in, loved, and **not** retail move-in ready. Traditional real estate agents often struggle with properties needing heavy repair, updating, or decluttering, causing long delays on the MLS. We specialize in real-world property solutions:

* **Direct "As-Is" Purchase (Primary Option):** We connect families directly with active, vetted institutional and local investors who buy properties in any condition. *No repairs, no prep, no public showings, and no need to clear out remaining contents.*  
* **Early Access to Sale Proceeds:** If a senior needs funds for community move-in deposits or monthly care fees *before* the home sale closes, we can often arrange an early advance of a portion of the sale proceeds through the buyer, so care can begin without taking on new debt.  
* **Traditional MLS Listing Support:** Reserved strictly for updated, turnkey homes where traditional retail marketing yields maximum value without delaying care timelines. 👉 [Explore Real Estate & Liquidity Solutions →](http:///real-estate)

### 📦 3\. Downsizing, Estate Sales & Move Management

Clearing a home of 30 to 50 years of accumulated belongings is often the biggest bottleneck in a senior transition.

* **Sorting & Inventorying:** Patient, respectful assistance deciding what to keep, gift, sell, or donate.  
* **Liquidation Services:** Managing estate sales or buyouts for valuable items to offset move costs.  
* **Turnkey Relocation:** Coordinating packing, transportation, unpacking, and setting up the new living space to feel like home on day one. 👉 [Explore Downsizing & Move Management →](http:///downsizing)

## Why Professionals Partner With Us

For attorneys, social workers, case managers, and senior living care directors, real estate and downsizing delays frequently stall care plans. We provide a single point of accountability to keep transitions moving smoothly.

| Referral Partner | The Bottleneck You Face | How Newtree Solves It |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Senior Living Directors** | Prospective residents are ready for private-pay or Board & Care placement, but their capital is tied up in an un-renovated home full of personal belongings. | We acquire the home "as-is" or arrange an early advance of the sale proceeds, freeing up capital so the resident can move into your community immediately. |
| **Social Workers & Case Managers** | Safe discharge stalls because Assisted Living Waiver (ALW) placements are not available in most rural Northern California counties, and setting up IHSS at home feels overwhelming to families. | We help assess whether aging in place with IHSS is workable. | **Elder Law & Probate Attorneys** | Clients face the reinstated Medi-Cal asset limit ($130,000 in 2026, scheduled to fall to $21,000 on July 1, 2027) and a 30-month look-back, while real property still needs to be resolved or care funded. | We provide direct "as-is" cash acquisition options, early advances of sale proceeds, and estate liquidations, timed to your client's Medi-Cal plan and documented to satisfy fiduciary and probate requirements. |
