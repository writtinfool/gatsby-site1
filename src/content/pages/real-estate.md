# Practical Property & Liquidity Solutions for Senior Transitions

Funding senior living care often hinges on unlocking equity from a long-time family home. However, traditional real estate methods aren't always designed for senior transition scenarios. Most buyers on the retail market want updated, turn-key homes, while senior-owned properties have often been lived in for decades and may need repairs, modernizing, or extensive decluttering.

At **Newtree Senior Transition Services**, we specialize in real-world property solutions tailored to the home’s actual condition, your family’s timeline, and your immediate care funding needs.

> ### 🛡️ Medi-Cal and Your Primary Home

> **You may not need to sell.** Under Medi-Cal rules, a primary residence is generally an exempt asset when determining eligibility for long-term care or IHSS, for example when the owner intends to return home or a spouse or qualifying relative lives there.
>
> With California's asset limit back in place ($130,000 for an individual in 2026, currently scheduled to drop to $21,000 on July 1, 2027) and a 30-month look-back on transfers, timing matters. Keep in mind that **selling an exempt home turns it into cash that does count** toward the limit. Before listing the home or accepting any offer, we encourage families to review the plan with their elder law attorney. When a sale is the right step, to fund private care, clear debts, or relocate, we help you sell as-is, quickly and transparently, on a timeline that fits your attorney's plan.
>
> **Every situation is different.** This is general information, not legal advice, and we are not the source for every circumstance. If you think the Medi-Cal asset rules may affect you or your family, we strongly encourage you to speak with a qualified elder law attorney before making decisions about savings, transfers, or the home.

## Our Three Real Estate Pathways

### Option A: Direct "As-Is" Investor Purchase (Primary & Most Common Solution)
If the house requires repairs, structural work, or modernizing—or if it is simply filled with decades of personal belongings—forcing it onto the traditional MLS market can lead to prolonged stress, price cuts, and failed escrows.

* **No Repairs, Updates, or Painting:** Sell the property in its exact present condition.
* **Leave What You Don't Want:** Take only the cherished personal items you want; our buyer network handles full cleanouts of all remaining furniture, trash, or debris.
* **Fast & Guaranteed Cash Closing:** Close in as little as 7–14 days (or on whatever timeline your family requires) so senior care funds are unlocked immediately.
* **Zero Public Showings:** Avoid open houses and constant walkthroughs by strangers.

### Option B: Transition & Bridge Financing
Wait times between deciding to move and closing a property transaction can create severe cash-flow bottlenecks for senior living deposits or monthly care fees.

* **Bridge Funding Access:** Access short-term liquidity options secured by real estate equity to cover community entrance fees, deposits, or medical bills while the property transaction is finalized.
* **Settlement Payoff:** Financed amounts are structured cleanly to be repaid directly upon settlement of the home sale.

### Option C: Traditional Retail Listing (For Pristine, Move-In Ready Homes)
If the home is already fully updated, modern, staged, and ready for an immediate retail family buyer, selling on the open retail market (MLS) may yield top dollar.

* **Senior-Focused MLS Marketing:** We partner with trusted local real estate agents across Northern California who understand senior transitions and can market turnkey homes efficiently without delaying care plans.
