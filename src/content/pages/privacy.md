# Privacy Policy

### Effective date: November 5, 2026

---

Newtree Senior Transition Services ("Newtree," "we," "us," or "our") helps seniors, families, and professional advisors in Northern California with senior living placement, real estate and liquidity solutions, and downsizing and relocation. Our work is personal, and we know you may be sharing sensitive information with us during a difficult time. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

This policy applies to transitions.newtreecap.com, our online forms, and the information you give us by phone, text, email, or in person.

---

## Information We Collect

**Information you give us.** When you call, text, email, or complete a form, you may provide:

- Your name, phone number, email address, and how you prefer to be contacted.
- Your relationship to the senior (for example, yourself, a parent, a spouse, or a client).
- General information about the senior's situation, such as the type of care being considered, preferred location, timing, and how care may be paid for (for example, private pay, long-term care insurance, veterans benefits, or Medi-Cal).
- Property information, such as the city or ZIP code, general condition, and your goals for the home.
- Information about belongings, moving plans, and services you would like help with.
- Anything else you choose to tell us in a message or conversation.

**Information from referral partners.** Attorneys, senior living staff, discharge planners, social workers, and case managers may refer a client or patient to us. They may give us that person's name, contact details, and a short description of their situation. We ask referral partners not to send medical records or other protected health information through our website.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request, and the date and time. We use it to keep the site secure and working. Our website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we do not build profiles of visitors. Our online forms are provided by Zoho, which may set cookies that the forms need in order to work.

We do not ask for Social Security numbers, bank or credit card numbers, or medical records through our website. Please don't include them in a form or message.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

- Respond to your inquiry and schedule a conversation or visit.
- Understand your needs and recommend appropriate options.
- Coordinate the services you ask us to provide, such as community tours, a property evaluation, or a move.
- Keep referral partners informed of key milestones, when the family or client has agreed.
- Send updates about your situation by phone, text, or email.
- Keep records required for our business, legal, and tax obligations.
- Protect the security of our website and prevent fraud or misuse.

We do not use your information for unrelated marketing, and we do not sell mailing lists.

---

## How We Share Information

We share personal information only as needed to help you, and only with people and organizations involved in your transition:

- **Senior living communities and care providers.** With your permission, we share relevant details with communities you are considering so they can confirm availability, fit, and admission requirements.
- **Real estate buyers, investors, agents, and financing partners.** With your permission, we share property information and contact details with the buyers, investors, licensed real estate agents, or lenders involved in a sale or bridge financing you have asked us to explore.
- **Moving, estate sale, donation, and cleanout providers.** We share the details these providers need to quote and perform the work you have requested.
- **Your referral partner.** If someone referred you to us, we may update them on progress, such as when a placement is secured, a property is under contract, or a move is complete, if you have agreed to this.
- **Service providers.** These companies help us operate, including website hosting (Cloudflare), online forms and records (Zoho), and email and phone services. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
- **Legal and safety reasons.** We may share information when required by law, to respond to legal process, or to protect the safety of a senior or another person.
- **Business transfers.** If our business is sold or reorganized, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.**

**How we are compensated.** Our placement guidance is free to families. We are paid through referral agreements with participating senior living communities when a move-in occurs. In real estate matters, we disclose our role, compensation, and any buyer relationships before you make a decision.

---

## Text Messages

If you give us your mobile number, you agree that we may text you about your inquiry and the services you have requested. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** at any time to stop receiving texts, or **HELP** for help. Agreeing to receive texts is not required in order to work with us.

**We do not share or sell mobile phone numbers or text-messaging consent with third parties for their own marketing purposes.**

---

## Health-Related Information

Families sometimes tell us about care needs, such as help with daily activities or memory care. We use this information only to find suitable options and coordinate your transition, and we share it only with the communities and providers involved, with your permission. Newtree is not a healthcare provider or health plan and is generally not covered by HIPAA. We still treat health-related details as sensitive and handle them with care.

---

## How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information for as long as we need it to help you and to meet our legal, tax, and record-keeping obligations. After that, we delete it or securely archive it. You may ask us to delete your information at any time; see "Your Choices and Rights" below.

---

## How We Protect Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to our website and access limited to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** information that is inaccurate.
- **Delete** your information, unless we need to keep it for a legal reason.
- **Opt out** of texts (reply STOP) or emails (ask us or use the unsubscribe link).
- **Limit** the use of sensitive information to what is needed to provide the services you requested.

**California residents.** California law, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), gives you the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to request access, correction, and deletion; to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither); and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. You may use an authorized agent, such as a family member with power of attorney, to make a request on your behalf. We may ask for information to verify your identity, or the agent's authority, before we act on a request.

**Do Not Track.** Our website does not track visitors across other websites, so we do not respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, although we do not sell or share personal information.

---

## Children's Privacy

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Links to Other Websites

Our website may link to other websites, such as senior living communities or partner services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please review their policies.
