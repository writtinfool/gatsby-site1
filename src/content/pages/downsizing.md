# Respectful Downsizing, Estate Liquidations & Move Management

Moving out of a home occupied for 30, 40, or 50 years is far more than just packing boxes—it is an emotional process of sorting through a lifetime of memories, achievements, and family history. 

For adult children and family caregivers, managing this physical workload alongside medical decisions and daily life can quickly lead to exhaustion. **Newtree Senior Transition Services** provides patient, hands-on downsizing and relocation support so your family can focus on supporting your loved one.

---

## How We Support Your Family’s Move

### 1. Compassionate Sorting & Decision-Making
* **Patient Guidance:** We work alongside seniors and family members at a comfortable pace, helping categorize items into keep, family gift, sell, donate, or recycle.
* **Floor Plan Spatial Matching:** We measure the floor plan of the new senior living apartment or residence to ensure selected furniture will fit comfortably and safely.

### 2. Estate Liquidations & Item Sale Management
* **Monetizing Unneeded Belongings:** We organize and oversee estate sales, online liquidations, or direct buyout options for valuable furniture, antiques, and collectibles.
* **Proceeds Applied to Move Costs:** Revenue generated from item sales can be applied directly to offset moving and transition expenses.
* **Donation & Eco-Friendly Cleanout:** We arrange pickups with local non-profits for usable goods and coordinate eco-friendly removal of remaining trash or debris.

### 3. Complete Turnkey Relocation Management
* **Vetted Senior Movers:** We hire and supervise professional, insured moving teams experienced in delicate senior moves.
* **Careful Packing & Labeling:** Belongings are packed with extreme care and clearly cataloged for precise placement in the new residence.
* **Day-One Move-In Setup:** We don't just drop boxes in the new room. We unpack, make beds, hang artwork, connect lamps, and arrange furniture so that when your loved one arrives on day one, the space instantly feels like home.
